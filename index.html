Ceylon Tea Estates & High Altitude Wellness

7 Days / 6 Nights Private Heritage & Wellness Escape

Tour Photo Gallery
Experience Overview: Experience the ultimate high-altitude luxury getaway amidst Sri Lanka's emerald tea mountains. Stay in historic 19th-century colonial tea master bungalows, enjoy personalized tea blending with world-renowned connoisseurs, fly in private helicopters over cloud forests, and indulge in organic mountain wellness spas.
Helicopter Transfers Private Butler Concierge Master Tea Blender Alpine Hydrotherapy Spa

Day-by-Day Journey

Day 1 - 2: VIP Arrival & Helicopter Scenic Flight to Tea Country

Fast-track arrival clearance at BIA Colombo. Board a private helicopter to Castlereagh Lake. Check-in to Ceylon Tea Trails restored colonial bungalow with dedicated butler service.

Day 3 - 4: Private Master Tea Blender & Horton Plains World's End Trek

Spend the morning picking tea leaves with artisan tea pluckers followed by a private tea tasting with a Master Blender. Next day, enjoy a private guided trek across Horton Plains with high-tea picnic at World's End.

Day 5 - 6: Mountain Hydrotherapy Spa & Fine-Dining Tea Dinners

Relaxation day with high-altitude organic spa therapies and hydro-baths. Evening features a 5-course tea-infused dinner prepared by an executive chef in the gardens.

Day 7: First-Class Scenic Train & Private Departure Transfer

Private observation train compartment through Ella gap. Private helicopter return to Colombo for VIP departure flight connection.

Wildlife Safaris & Eco-Luxury Glamping

5 Days / 4 Nights Tented Jungle Opulence

Tour Photo Gallery
Experience Overview: Step into Sri Lanka's untamed wilderness without compromising on world-class luxury. Nestled between Yala National Park and the Indian Ocean, experience luxury cocoon glamping, customized 4x4 leopard tracking safaris with expert naturalists, and atmospheric bush dinners under starry skies.
Wild Coast Luxury Tent Master Naturalist Guide Private 4x4 Leopard Safari Sunset Bush BBQ & Champagne

Day-by-Day Journey

Day 1: Seaplane Transfer & Jungle Cocoon Welcome

Transfer via seaplane directly to Yala water-drome. Check-in at Wild Coast Tented Lodge. Sundowner champagne on the boulder coast.

Day 2 - 3: Leopard & Sloth Bear Tracking with Head Naturalist

Early morning VIP 4x4 game drives in private zones of Yala. Afternoon bush walk along park boundaries, followed by a private star-lit bush dinner.

Day 4: Udawalawe Elephant Sanctuary & Wilderness Spa

Visit the Elephant Transit Home with private sanctuary access. Return to camp for evening outdoor copper-tub hydro-spa treatments.

Day 5: Dawn Game Drive & Direct Helicopter Return

Final sunrise game drive to spot wild leopards and peacocks. Private helicopter transfer directly to BIA airport for departure flight.

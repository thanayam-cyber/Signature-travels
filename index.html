Day-by-Day Journey

Day 1 - 2: VIP Arrival & Helicopter Flight to Tea Country

Fast-track arrival clearance at BIA. Board a private helicopter to Castlereagh Lake. Check-in to Ceylon Tea Trails with dedicated butler service.

Day 3 - 4: Master Tea Blender & Horton Plains Trek

Pick tea leaves with artisan pluckers followed by tea tasting with a Master Blender. Next day, enjoy a private guided trek across Horton Plains.

Day 5 - 6: Mountain Hydrotherapy Spa & Fine-Dining

Relaxation day with high-altitude organic spa therapies. Evening features a 5-course tea-infused dinner prepared by an executive chef.

Day 7: Scenic Train & Private Departure Transfer

Private observation train compartment through Ella. Helicopter return to Colombo for VIP departure flight connection.
